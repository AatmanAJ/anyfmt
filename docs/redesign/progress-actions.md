# Redesign: progress cards, toasts, menu bar, Float shelf, Move chooser

Area notes for the phase 2 redesign (PLAN-PHASE2 §2). The target is the approved
board, `docs/screens/after/direction/5-progress-cards-dark.png`. "Before" renders
are in `docs/screens/before/`; "after" renders are the files of the same name in
`docs/screens/`, regenerated by `make test` / `make gallery`.

## What changed

### Progress cards (`Sources/Jobs/UI`)

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Translucent popover vibrancy, 14 pt radius, two stacked shadows | Solid charcoal tile (`Semantic.tile`), `Shape.tile`, one `Elevation.float` shadow; a hairline only in light mode or with Increased Contrast |
| Lead | File icon or Quick Look thumbnail | The big display numeral (Inter Display Black, `displayL`, −3 % tracking, tabular digits) |
| Running | "Converting to JPG", bar, "42% · About 12 s left" | Numeral **48%**; headline "interview.mov → MP4"; detail "About 20 s left"; quiet **Cancel** |
| Queued / cancelling | Same as running | Numeral dimmed (`textDimDisplay`, display size only) + "Waiting…" / "Cancelling…" in words |
| Done | Green-ish check, file name, "Saved beside the original" | Numeral is the size change (**−62%**), else the time it took (**4.8 s**), else the file count; check glyph (cobalt, white tick) + output name; "Done · 2.4 MB · from 6.3 MB"; **Show in Finder** appears on hover (the whole card still reveals) |
| Failed | Red badge on the icon, message, pills | Flame warning glyph in the numeral slot, failure title, the file, the human message in full, quiet **Copy details** / **Close**, cobalt **Retry** pill |
| "+3 more" | Small 30 pt bar | Same tile language: display **+3**, "more jobs", "3 running · 41%" |
| Progress bar | Iris capsule with a linear fade | `BandGradient(bands: Band.cobalt)`: hard-edged bands; flat cobalt + outline with Increased Contrast or Reduce Transparency |
| Width | 288 pt | 420 pt (the board's proportions), 16 pt padding, 116 pt numeral column, 10 pt between stacked cards |

Copy is longest-first: the detail line shows the full wording when it fits and a
compact one when it doesn't ("4 of 12 · 2 already JPG · About 31 s left" →
"4 of 12 · 31 s left"; "Done · 2.4 MB · from 6.3 MB" → "2.4 MB · from 6.3 MB").

**Copy details** now goes through `ProgressDiagnostics.report`: the app/macOS line,
then `Engines: FFmpeg 9.0.2 · resvg 0.48.1` (the shipped binaries from
`BundledEngines`), the job, its inputs/route/options, the engine's `… exited N`
line and the **last 40 lines of its stderr** (with a "[… N earlier stderr lines]"
marker), and the home folder rewritten to `~` everywhere: plain paths and
`file://` URLs, percent-encoded or not, whole path components only (so
`/Users/sam` never eats `/Users/samantha`). Tested in `ProgressDiagnosticsTests`.

**Placement** is unchanged in behaviour and still unit-tested: the newest card's
top edge sits 26 pt under the drop point, on the display the drop happened on,
the column pushed fully inside that display's visible frame.

### Toasts (`Sources/App/Integration/ToastHUD.swift`)

Charcoal pill with an icon and semibold words; when the words don't fit on one
line (measured, not guessed) it becomes a charcoal tile and wraps to three lines.
Success: cobalt check. Info (e.g. "Link copied"): the caller's symbol in cobalt.
Error: flame warning triangle, a longer stay (3.5 s vs 1.6 s), and VoiceOver
hears "Error: …" at high priority. Renders: `toast-states-{dark,light}.png`,
`toast-high-contrast.png`.

### Menu bar (`Sources/App/Integration/StatusItemController.swift`)

Still a native `NSMenu` with the `MenuBarIcon` template image. Reordered so what
you came for is first: Open Wheel for Finder Selection, Show/Hide Float Shelf,
Recent Conversions ▸ — then Settings… ⌘,, Pro Licence & Account…, Check for
Updates…, About — then Quit ⌘Q. Every item has a template SF Symbol (the menu
tints them, so selection, dark mode and Increased Contrast just work). Recents
gets a "Show in Finder" section label, `~`-abbreviated tooltips, and the empty
state reads "Nothing converted yet". ⌥ still reveals Wheel Gallery and Show Welcome….

### Float shelf, Move chooser (`Sources/Actions`)

- `ActionCardView`: no `NSVisualEffectView` any more. Solid charcoal tile,
  `Shape.tile` radius (was 18), one floating shadow, hairline edge in light mode.
  The drag-over "glow" (iris border + blurred shadow) is now a plain **2 pt
  cobalt outline** (the board's "locked" language; glowing borders are banned).
- Shelf counts use the display face: **5** items (Inter Display, `displayM`),
  left-aligned with the kinds line under it. The pile's iris count badge is gone
  (it repeated the number right under it).
- Thumbnails are square-cornered prints with a paper border (only the tile and
  pill radii are allowed; a print has neither). Finder icons keep a contact shadow.
- Empty shelf: dashed drop zone, tray glyph top-left, "Drop files here / Files
  stay in place." bottom-left; while files are over it the dash, glyph and outline
  go cobalt and the title says "Drop to add". New renders: `float-drop-{light,dark}.png`.
- Grid: hover is the raised charcoal step (`Semantic.raised`), `Shape.tile`.
- Move chooser: charcoal tile; the selected row is a **cobalt pill** with all-white
  text (the old 78 %/70 % white detail and key text fell below AA at caption size);
  folder names bold, locations and number keys in `textSecondary`; "Choose Folder…"
  glyph in cobalt text colour.
- Sample thumbnails in `ActionScreens` are redrawn from the palette (heat-band
  sunset, cobalt lagoon, night skyline, document), backdrops are page black / light
  grey instead of the purple-teal wash.
- Drag-out, promises, Option-to-keep, AirDrop, Terminal and clipboard behaviour
  are untouched; their outcomes surface through the new toasts.

## Motion and accessibility

- Cards in: `Curve.glide` (opacity + 8 pt drop from the pointer); out:
  `Curve.exit`; restack slides on `glide`; button presses `Curve.snap`. Toasts,
  shelf resize/hide, grid reflow and the Move chooser use the same curves.
  Reduce Motion turns every move into a `reducedCrossfade` fade.
- Reduce Transparency: nothing is translucent any more; the cobalt band goes flat.
- Increased Contrast: secondary text becomes primary, tiles and quiet buttons get
  a visible outline, the bar gets an outline and goes flat, dim numerals lift to
  `textSecondary`.
- State never rides on colour: success = check glyph + "Done"; failure = warning
  glyph + "Couldn’t …" + message; queued/cancelling = dim numeral + the word.
- VoiceOver: each card reads "headline, numeral, detail, message" ("clip.gif, 62%
  smaller, Done · 2.4 MB · from 6.3 MB"). The bar exposes "Progress" with a
  percent value. Running progress is announced **at most once per quarter and no
  more than every 8 s, only while VoiceOver is on, at low priority**; finishes and
  failures are announced once.
- Contrast (sRGB): white on charcoal 16.5:1; `ink400` secondary on charcoal 5.9:1;
  `ink300` on the light tile 6.0:1; flame on charcoal 4.9:1 and ember on the light
  tile 5.2:1 (failure glyph and toast glyph); cobalt-500 behind white 5.0:1 (Retry,
  selected row); dim numerals only ever at 40 pt (`ink500`: 3.2:1 on charcoal, 4.6:1 on the light tile; the semantic light `textDimDisplay` is 2.5:1 there, so the light card uses `ink500` instead).

## Polish list (not done here)

1. **Engine command line in Copy details.** `ProcessRunner` (Engine area) throws
   `engineFailed(name, status, stderr)` without the argv, so the report has the
   route, options, exit line and stderr tail but not the literal command. A
   three-line change there (append the redacted argv to `detail`) would complete
   it; `ProgressDiagnostics` already redacts whatever it gets.
2. A "1080p"-style spec in the running detail (the board shows "1080p · about
   20 s left"). Needs the job to expose its key option summary; today the detail
   is the ETA alone.
3. The numeral could tick with `.contentTransition(.numericText())` on macOS 14+;
   kept static on 13 for parity.
4. The shelf's empty-state dash in light mode uses `textSecondary` (6.7:1); it
   reads a touch heavy next to the hairline tile and could move to a dedicated
   `dropZone` token at 3:1.
5. Feel check on hardware: card width 420 pt is the board's; on a 13" display with
   five cards and the summary the column is ~560 pt tall and will be pushed up
   from a low drop point (by design, but worth a look).
6. `ProgressPresenterTests.testHoverPausesAutoClose` reads real tracking areas, so
   a pointer resting where the test card opens can flip its hover state; it passes
   on rerun. Pre-existing; the wider card makes it slightly more likely.
